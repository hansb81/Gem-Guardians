Gem Guardians 계정 삭제

Team SDB · 2026년 11월 15일

1. 앱에서 지우기 (바로 삭제)

  1. 게임을 열고 메뉴 오른쪽 위 설정을 누릅니다.
  2. 계정 > 맨 아래 계정 삭제를 누릅니다.
  3. 안내를 읽고 입력 칸에 삭제를 입력한 뒤 [삭제]를 누릅니다.

게스트(계정을 연결하지 않은 경우)와 Google 계정을 연결한 경우 모두 같은 방법입니다. 삭제는 바로 처리되며 되돌릴 수 없습니다. 인터넷에 연결돼 있어야 합니다.

2. 앱을 이미 지웠다면 (이메일로 요청)

lorddark01@gmail.com으로 아래 중 하나를 적어 보내 주세요.

  • 플레이어 코드(게임 설정 > 게임 정보에 보이는 8자리, 또는 길게 눌러 복사한 전체 코드)
  • 랭킹 닉네임
  • 연결한 Google 계정 이메일(그 주소로 보내 주시면 확인이 빠릅니다)

본인 확인 뒤 7일 안에 삭제하고 회신합니다.

Delete your Gem Guardians account

Team SDB · November 15, 2026

1. In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the game and tap Settings at the top right of the menu.
  2. Tap Account > Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Read the notice, type DELETE in the box and tap [Delete].

This works the same for guests and for players who linked a Google account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. An internet connection is required.

2. If you already uninstalled the app (by email)

Email lorddark01@gmail.com with one of the following:

  • Your player code (8 characters shown in Settings > Game Info, or the full code copied with a long press)
  • Your ranking nickname
  • The email of the linked Google account (sending from that address speeds up verification)

We will delete your data within 7 days after verifying the request and reply to you.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted (immediately)Kept
Your player account and sign-in data (email and name of a linked Google or Apple account)
Game progress (floors, gold, diamonds, items, statue levels) - the cloud save and its earlier copies on our server, and the save on your device
Ranking records and nickname; attendance, mail, coupon and reward claim records
Your customer record at our payment service (RevenueCat) 		Purchase records (order ID, product, time): kept for 5 years without your name (unlinked from your player) as required by Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, then deleted
Device settings such as sound and language (only on your device; removed when you uninstall)

There are no subscriptions. Consumable items already purchased (such as diamonds) are not refunded. See the privacy policy for details.
