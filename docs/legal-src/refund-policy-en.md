# Gem Guardians Refund Policy (English)

> Translation for reference. If this and the Korean version differ, the **Korean version prevails**. Not legal advice.

Effective date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

**1. Scope.** Applies to diamonds, packs, passes and Ad Removal bought with money. Free items are not refundable.

**2. Key points.** Payments are processed by the Store, so money is returned through the Store. A refund reclaims everything from that purchase (diamonds, items, bonuses, effects). Losing unlinked guest data is not a refund reason; permanent products can be restored via "Restore Purchases". Request refunds before deleting your account.

**3. Cancellation within 7 days.** Within 7 days of purchase, if **nothing from that purchase has been used** (all diamonds incl. bonus and all items can be reclaimed), you get a full refund with no fee. Cancellation is restricted (and shown before purchase) when: any item in a bundle was used; any diamonds from the purchase, including the first-purchase bonus, were used; an instantly applied product was used (an ad-free reward claimed, or the 4th slot used in battle); pass rewards were claimed (see pass termination); or 7 days have passed. If a product differs from its description or was not delivered properly, you may cancel within 3 months of receipt or 30 days of discovering it, regardless of use.

**4. Products.**
| Product | Price (KRW) | Full refund within 7 days if |
|---|---|---|
| Diamonds (6 tiers, ×2 on first purchase) | 1,200–119,000 | all diamonds incl. bonus unused |
| Starter Pack / Boss Pack / Chapter Pack | 1,200 / 5,900 / 1,900 | no item in the pack used |
| 4th Slot Pack | 2,900 | slot never used in battle, rainbows unused |
| Ad Removal | 6,500 | no ad-free reward claimed, diamonds unused |
| Weekly / Monthly Pass | 5,900 / 11,000 | no instant or daily rewards claimed |

First-purchase bonus: reclaimed together with the base diamonds on refund, and not granted again on repurchase.

Pass termination after claiming: refund = price × daily share × (remaining days ÷ total days) − 10% of price (rounded down to 10 KRW, minimum 0). Daily share: Weekly 89%, Monthly 91%. Example: Weekly Pass, 3 days claimed → 5,900 × 0.89 × 4/7 − 590 = 2,410 KRW. Missed days add up on your next claim (up to 3 days after it ends); unclaimed rewards expire after that. No fee for days missed because of our error. Passes bought on the App Store cannot be partially refunded by us; request a refund from Apple, which decides whether and how much to refund.

**5. How to request.**
- Google Play: within 48 hours, request in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history); after that, email us.
- ONE store: ONE store purchase history / customer center, or email us.
- App Store: request directly from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund Apple purchases.
- Toss: email us.
Include your player ID (Settings > Game Info), store and order number, purchase date, product and reason. We review within 3 business days and cancel through the Store where possible; the money arrives on the Store/card/carrier schedule.

**6. Minors.** Purchases by users under 19 without guardian consent can be cancelled regardless of the 7-day limit or use. Send proof of guardianship (e.g. family relation certificate) and the guardian's ID with sensitive numbers masked. Cancellation may be refused if the minor used deception or spent within a permitted allowance.

**7. Service termination.** Remaining pass days are refunded pro-rata without fee; unused paid diamonds/packs bought within 90 days before the announcement are refunded. Permanent products are usable until the termination date and are not refunded.

**8. Overcharges.** Duplicate charges or undelivered items are refunded in full or delivered. Try "Restore Purchases" first.

**9. Refund abuse.** If refunded content was already used, we may reclaim remaining items and deduct diamonds down to 0, and may restrict accounts that repeatedly abuse refunds or use others' payment methods.
